The 4-step booking flow

  1. Choose your arrival airport and package on the booking form.
  2. Enter your flight date, flight number, and passenger count.
  3. Add your contact details and pay using the payment option shown on the form.
  4. An agent confirms your booking within 2 working hours and meets you at the gate with a name sign.

How far ahead to book

Booking in advance is generally cheaper and gives the agent more time to confirm availability, so it is the best option when your travel dates are fixed. Same-day or next-day service is sometimes possible at SGN and HAN, but availability is more limited and package choices can shrink. During holiday peaks - especially Tet and Christmas - packages can sell out, so reserve as early as possible for those dates. If you are still deciding whether the service fits your arrival, check real queue times by airport first.

What you need ready

  • A passport valid for at least 6 months.
  • Your flight number and scheduled arrival time.
  • Your visa approval letter or a printed e-visa, if applicable to your trip.
  • Your hotel name for the arrival card.

Enter passenger and flight details carefully so the agent can match the booking to the correct arrival. If a flight changes after booking, pass the updated details through the contact method included with your confirmation.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to book fast track in Vietnam?

Arrival Priority Meet and Greet starts at 880,000-1,130,000 VND per person depending on airport (roughly US$34-43); departure send-off runs 880,000-1,250,000 VND. Business Class and VIP tiers run higher - see the full price tables above.

Can I book on the same day I fly?

Sometimes at SGN and HAN, but availability is limited and choices shrink. Advance booking is cheaper and guarantees an agent on your flight - book as early as you can for Tet and Christmas dates.

What do I need to complete the booking?

Passport valid 6+ months, flight number and arrival time, visa approval letter or printed e-visa if applicable, and your hotel name for the arrival card.

How do I find my agent after landing?

An agent confirms within 2 working hours and meets you at the arrival gate holding a sign with your name. Flight changed? Send the update through your confirmation's contact channel.
