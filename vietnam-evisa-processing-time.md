## Processing time at a glance

| Situation | Typical wait |

| Clean application, normal week | 3-4 working days |

| Submitted Friday / before holiday | 5-7 days |

| Photo or name-order problem | 7-10 days or returned for correction |

| Tet and long public holidays | Up to 2 weeks |

## Why the official "3 working days" slips

The 3-day figure is the service target, not a guarantee - and it counts working days in Vietnam's timezone. Three things stretch it:

- Weekends and public holidays. The Immigration Department does not process on Saturdays, Sundays, or Vietnamese holidays. A Thursday submission before a holiday week can sit for 10+ days.

- Photo rejections. The portrait photo must be front-facing, glasses-free, on a plain background, in the exact ratio the portal specifies. Non-standard photos are the single biggest cause of delays and resubmission requests.

- Name-order mismatches. The portal asks for surname and given name in a specific order. Applicants who reverse them, or whose names contain accents the form drops, get flagged for manual review.

## How to check your status

- Save the registration code shown when you submit - the portal does not email it reliably.

- Use the "Search" function on evisa.gov.vn with the code, your email, and date of birth.

- Status moves from "Processing" to "Granted Visa" with a PDF download link. Print it or keep the PDF offline - the counter wants the document, not a screenshot of the portal.

## What actually speeds things up

- Apply early. Two weeks before departure removes nearly all stress. The e-visa is valid from the date you choose, so applying early costs nothing.

- Submit a technically clean file. Correct photo spec, name in passport order, exact entry port. A clean application clears in the stated window far more often.

- Double-check the entry port. The visa is tied to the port you name (e.g. "Noi Bai Int'l Airport - Ha Noi"). Landing at a different airport with the wrong port on the visa creates a problem fast track cannot fix.

- Reapply rather than wait. If an application is stuck past 10 days, a fresh clean application often lands faster than the correction loop.

## Where fast track fits (and where it does not)

Fast track is an airport service - it cannot influence visa approval. What it does: once your e-visa is granted, an agent meets you at the arrival gate and escorts you through the priority immigration lane, cutting a 45-90 minute queue to about 10-15 minutes. If the e-visa itself is still pending, fix the visa first - no airport service can substitute for it.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does a Vietnam e-visa take to process?** — Officially 3 working days. In practice 3-5 working days for clean applications; up to 10 days or a correction request if the photo or name order is off, or if submitted before a holiday.

**Can I get a Vietnam e-visa faster than 3 working days?** — There is no official express option. The practical speed-up is submitting a technically clean application early so it is not returned for correction.

**Why is my e-visa still processing after a week?** — Usually a photo failing automated checks, a name-order mismatch, a holiday pause, or manual review. Check status with your registration code; past 10 days, reapply cleanly.

**My e-visa is pending and my flight is in 2 days - what now?** — You cannot board without the visa. Keep checking the portal (approvals sometimes land last-minute), call the Immigration Department hotline, or move the flight. Fast track cannot replace a visa.
