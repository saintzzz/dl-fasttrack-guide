## The thing to understand first: Avilink is B2B

Avilink does not run a consumer booking site - its own FAQ says you book "through agencies, the Sales department or official booking channels." Its volume comes from distribution: travel agencies and tour operators, OTAs like Trip.com (where its Tan Son Nhat fast-track arrival lists from ~$25.41), and bank partnerships ( ABBank app users get 20% off select packages). The website is a polished brochure; the actual commerce happens inside other companies' checkouts.

## What that means for you

| Factor | How it plays out |

| Booking flow | Depends on channel: OTA checkout, agency invoice, or WhatsApp/hotline (+84 563 99 22 99). Not a 5-minute self-serve flow. |

| Airports | SGN, HAN, DAD, PQC - four international airports. |

| Service scope | Fast track, meet & assist, VIP tiers, lounge access, transfers - the full corporate-grade stack, aimed at business and VIP travelers. |

## Where Avilink is strong

- Scale and polish - 200k+ passengers/year, 24/7 ops, professional uniformed staff; the service quality bar that smaller operators measure themselves against.

- Everywhere you already buy - inside Trip.com, agency packages, bank apps. If your agent or OTA offers Avilink at checkout, it is a vetted option.

- Corporate depth - group arrivals, event logistics, multi-flight coordination that direct sites do not handle.

## Where it is weaker

- No direct checkout - you cannot just visit the site, pick a package and pay. Expect a quote loop through a partner or hotline.

- Opaque pricing - no published rates; what you pay depends entirely on which partner sells it to you.

- Support flows through the seller - changes and issues go through whoever took your booking, adding a hop.

## The practical verdict

If Avilink shows up inside a checkout you are already using (Trip.com, your agency, ABBank), take it - the underlying operation is strong. But if you are starting cold and want fast track booked and confirmed tonight, a direct provider with a real booking engine gets you there faster: live price, written confirmation SLA, and payment to your agent on the day. ## Frequently asked questions

**Can I book Avilink online directly?** — No self-serve checkout - book via partner agencies, OTAs like Trip.com (~$25 SGN arrival), bank promos (ABBank -20%), or their hotline/WhatsApp.

**What does Avilink cost?** — No public price list; partner-dependent. Trip.com shows ~$25.41 for SGN arrival fast track.

**Is Avilink legit?** — Yes - HCMC-based, ~200k passengers/year, 500+ partners, four airports. The highest-volume operator in Vietnam.

**Avilink vs a direct provider?** — Avilink = buy inside someone else's product. Direct providers like AAL = own checkout, live price, 2h confirmation. Cold-start bookings are faster direct.

**Why can’t I find an Avilink price list?** — Because Avilink prices per channel - the OTA, agency or bank that sells you the service sets the retail tag. Trip.com currently lists SGN arrival around $25. This is normal for B2B operators; it just means comparison shopping happens inside each partner’s checkout, not on avilink.vn.

**Is the Avilink agent different from a direct provider’s?** — The service on the ground is similar - a named agent, a signboard, a fast-track lane. What changes between channels is who you pay, who confirms you, and who you call if nobody shows. Booking direct means the operator owns your booking end-to-end.
