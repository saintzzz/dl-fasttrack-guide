## The three departure queues

| Queue | Peak wait |

| Check-in / bag drop | 20-45 min (worse if no kiosk access) |

| Security screening | 15-30 min |

| Exit immigration | 15-40 min at morning banks |

| Total standing time | ~60-90 min at peak |

The morning international bank - roughly 7-11am at HAN and SGN - is the worst window: dozens of wide-bodies check in and clear out within a few hours.

## How departure fast track works

- Meet at the terminal. The agent meets you at the international departures entrance or your airline's check-in row - the confirmation gives the exact point.

- Check-in assist. They walk you to the right counter, help with bag drop and any document checks (visa rules for your destination are a common snag).

- Priority security. You use the fast lane instead of the general queue.

- Priority exit immigration. The crew/diplomat lane again - minutes instead of the 15-40 minute main queue.

- Gate or lounge. Standard service leaves you airside; VIP tiers walk you to a lounge or all the way to boarding.

## When it pays off

- Morning international peaks - 7-11am departures from HAN and SGN face the full three-queue stack.

- Tet and holiday exodus - the days around Vietnamese holidays are the busiest of the year, with departure queues rivaling arrival ones.

- Late arrival at the airport - traffic, a late hotel checkout, a long transfer from Ha Long or Ninh Binh. When the clock is the enemy, removing queue risk is the whole product.

- Kids, elderly parents, or a lot of luggage - shepherding a group through three queues solo is genuinely hard work.

- Business travelers on fixed schedules - a missed flight costs more than the service.

Skip-it case: a quiet afternoon or late-evening departure outside holiday season - all three queues can total under 25 minutes.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does departure fast track include?** — Terminal meet, check-in/bag-drop help, priority security, priority exit immigration, escort to gate or lounge. Three queues become one guided walk.

**When is it worth it?** — Morning international peaks (7-11am at HAN/SGN), Tet and holidays, late airport arrival, traveling with kids/elderly, or a fixed business schedule. Quiet afternoon departures often don't need it.

**How long are departure queues?** — Check-in 20-45 min, security 15-30, exit immigration 15-40 at peak - up to ~90 minutes combined at morning banks.

**How early should I arrive?** — 3 hours is the standard advice at peak. With fast track, 2-2.5 hours is usually comfortable since queue risk is removed.
