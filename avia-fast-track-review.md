## Where AVIA is strong

- Real online booking - pick package, see price, pay. No quote-by-WhatsApp dance.

- Four airports - SGN, HAN, DAD and Phu Quoc , which several rivals (including AAL) do not serve.

- Bundle math - transfer + eSIM + fast track in one order saves a separate booking.

- Volume signal - 355 on-site reviews at 4.9 stars and big served-counts per package.

## Where it is weaker

- Published prices are "from" prices - the site itself notes final quotes can differ; expect the real number at checkout to drift up from the headline $39.

- No public confirmation SLA - nothing like AAL's explicit "confirmed within 2 working hours" commitment on the page.

- Transit pricing is steep - connecting fast track lists at $172, roughly 4x the arrival entry tier.

## AVIA vs booking AAL direct

These are the two real online checkouts in the market. AVIA's edge: $4 cheaper headline, Phu Quoc coverage, eSIM/transfer bundles. AAL's edge: defined 2-hour confirmation SLA, fully transparent per-step package pages in EN/VI/ZH, and the VIP B tier where staff complete immigration while you walk through without stopping. For SGN, HAN or DAD compare both live prices - they are close enough that availability and confirmation speed matter more than a few dollars. For Phu Quoc , AAL is not an option - AVIA is the pick.
