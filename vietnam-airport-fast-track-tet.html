Landing in Vietnam during Tet

Tet is Vietnam's biggest travel surge of the year - and the airport pain is real, especially if your itinerary includes a domestic leg. Here is what to expect and how to land smoothly.

Last updated November 2026 · Price ranges are market-typical; see live pricing per date on the booking page

What Tet does to Vietnam's airports

Tet Nguyen Dan (Lunar New Year) is the largest human migration in Vietnam's calendar. In 2027, Tet falls on 6 February, with the heaviest travel running roughly 30 January - 10 February. The airport picture splits in two:

  • Domestic terminals explode - Tan Son Nhat domestic is famously chaotic in the days before Tet, with check-in and security lines spilling outside. Noi Bai domestic is similar.
  • International arrivals rise steadily - overseas Vietnamese (Viet Kieu) arriving for Tet add load to immigration halls in the 7-10 days before the holiday, especially on flights from the US, Australia and Europe.

Where it actually hurts

  • International-to-domestic connections - the worst single scenario: landing at SGN international then fighting the domestic terminal for a flight to Da Nang or Hue during Tet week. An escorted transfer across terminals saves real time and stress.
  • Departure day - leaving Vietnam right after Tet means crowded check-in, long security, and packed exit-immigration. A departure escort pays for itself most clearly on these days.
  • Ground transport on Tet Eve - taxis and Grab thin out drastically on the afternoon of Tet Eve and the first morning. Pre-booked pickup or a meet-and-greet with transfer avoids being stranded.

Planning checklist for a Tet arrival

  • Book fast track 2-4 weeks out - holiday slots are the first to fill because each booking needs a named agent on your specific flight.
  • Have your e-visa done before departure - visa-on-arrival queues add a full second wait during the Tet rush; see the e-visa processing time guide.
  • Leave buffer for domestic connections - treat anything under 2.5 hours as risky during Tet week without an escorted transfer.
  • Pre-arrange the last mile - hotel pickup or a driver beats the taxi queue, especially on Tet Eve and the first two days.

Cost at Tet

Expect standard pricing (roughly US$20-50 standard, US$40-80+ VIP per person) with possible holiday surcharges at some providers on Tet Eve and the first two days. The real Tet constraint is capacity, not price - see typical costs for baseline numbers.

Is Tet the worst time to arrive in Vietnam?

For domestic terminals, yes - Tan Son Nhat and Noi Bai domestic halls hit their yearly peak in the final week before Tet. International arrivals are busy but less extreme; the bigger issue is connecting to a domestic leg during the Tet rush.

How early should I book fast track for Tet?

Two to four weeks ahead is sensible for the peak window (roughly 10 days before to 5 days after Tet Eve). Fast track requires a named agent per flight, and peak Tet slots are the first to fill.

Are airport services open during Tet itself?

Yes - airports and fast track services operate normally through Tet, though ground transport (taxis, Grab) thins out on Tet Eve and the first day, making pre-arranged pickup more valuable.
