Where VJP wins

  • True VVIP product - $300 buys non-stop escort through everything; most competitors cap out lower or fuzz the top tier.
  • Granular pricing - published table with airport-specific deltas (SGN surcharges) instead of a single "from" number.
  • Four airports - SGN, HAN, DAD, PQC including Phu Quoc.

Where it loses ground

  • No instant checkout - booking runs through contact form; the beautiful price table is informational, not shoppable directly.
  • Airline restrictions - departure Priority not available for VietJet at SGN/DAD, and Premium is business-class-only at SGN - check your ticket first.
  • "Base rate" caveat - night/early-morning and special requests may add charges beyond the table.

The verdict

VJP is the pick when you want the ceiling product - non-stop VVIP at $300, or need Phu Quoc. At the practical $35-50 end it trades within a few dollars of rivals; there the decider is booking flow, and form-based quote loops lose to instant checkout when time is short.
