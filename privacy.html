Privacy policy

Short version: this is a read-only guide. We do not run accounts, take bookings, or collect personal information on this domain.

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

  • Aggregated usage statistics - page views and traffic sources, collected through standard web analytics. This data is not tied to your identity.
  • Email you send us - if you write to us, we keep the correspondence to answer and to fix guide errors.

What we do not collect

  • No account sign-ups, no forms asking for personal details, no newsletter list
  • No payment information - we never take bookings or payments on this site
  • No passport or visa data - we do not process applications

Referral links

Booking links take you to AAL Fast Track (fasttrack.aal.vn) carrying a referral parameter. If you continue there, that site's privacy policy and terms apply - they collect what they need to deliver the service. We receive only a commission confirmation, not your personal booking details.

Cookies

This site may set minimal, functional cookies (for example, analytics measurement). We do not use advertising trackers and do not run retargeting pixels on this domain.

Your rights

If you have emailed us and want the correspondence deleted, or have any privacy question, write to linhld@vieschool.com and we will handle it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version replaces this page and the date above is revised.
