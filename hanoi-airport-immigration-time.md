## The honest numbers

| Arrival window | Typical immigration wait |

| Early morning bank (5-8am) | 45-75 min |

| Midday quiet (10am-3pm) | 20-40 min |

| Evening bank (7-10pm) | 45-75 min |

| Late night (after 11pm) | 15-30 min |

| Fast track, any window | ~10-15 min |

These are immigration-only times at Terminal 2 - add baggage claim and customs and a "normal" arrival takes 60-90 minutes door to curb on a bad window.

## Why the queue has two peaks

Noi Bai's international schedule is bimodal:

- The 5-8am bank - European long-hauls (London, Paris, Frankfurt, Doha, Dubai) land within a tight window. Several thousand passengers hit the same hall within an hour.

- The 7-10pm bank - Seoul, Tokyo, Taipei, Hong Kong, Bangkok and other East Asian flights arrive in a dense evening wave.

Between the banks the hall is manageable. Land outside them and you can be through in half an hour.

## What makes your wait longer than the table

- Visa-on-arrival approval letter - you must stop at the Landing Visa counter first for the sticker, a second queue that adds 20-40 minutes at peak.

- Document problems - a missing e-visa printout or a passport validity question sends you aside while the line continues.

- Weekends and holidays - Tet and Vietnamese public holidays stack domestic and international peaks on top of each other.

- Seating position - arriving at the hall with two other wide-bodies vs 15 minutes after them is the difference between 30 and 60 minutes.

## How to estimate your own wait

- Check your scheduled landing time against the two banks above.

- Add 20-40 minutes if you are on a visa-on-arrival letter instead of an e-visa.

- Add a margin for Tet/holiday travel dates.

- Anything over ~45 minutes is where standing in the hall after a long flight starts to genuinely hurt - that is the threshold where fast track earns its price.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long is the immigration queue at Hanoi airport?** — 30-60 minutes typically at Noi Bai T2; 45-75 minutes during the 5-8am and 7-10pm arrival banks. VoA letter holders can add a second 20-40 minute queue.

**What are the worst times to arrive?** — The two daily banks: 5-8am (European flights) and 7-10pm (East Asian flights). Late morning/early afternoon is usually the calmest.

**Does an e-visa make the queue shorter?** — A little - you skip the Landing Visa desk. But the main immigration queue is where most of the wait is, and e-visa holders join it like everyone else.

**Is there any way to skip the queue?** — Fast track - agent meets you at the gate and escorts you through the priority lane, ~10-15 minutes total. There is no free priority lane for tourists at HAN.
