About VieSchool Fast Track

An independent Vietnam airport arrival guide, written in Hanoi by people who fly in and out of these airports regularly.

Last updated November 2026

Who runs this site

VieSchool Fast Track is a travel-content project run by the VieSchool team in Hanoi, Vietnam. We are a content site, not a government agency and not the airport authority. Everything here is practical, traveler-facing information about arriving at and departing from Vietnam's three international gateways - Noi Bai (HAN), Tan Son Nhat (SGN) and Da Nang (DAD).

What we publish

  • Realistic immigration wait times and how they vary by airport, hour and season
  • Step-by-step guides for e-visa, visa-on-arrival, arrival cards and airport procedures
  • Honest assessments of paid services - fast track, meet & greet, SIM pickup - including when they are not worth paying for
  • Airport-by-airport details: counters, desks, prices and the queues nobody warns you about

How we research

  • Primary sources first: the official Vietnam e-visa portal, immigration procedures, and airport-published information
  • Real arrival reports and timing data collected from travelers and our own arrivals at HAN, SGN and DAD
  • Pages are re-verified and dated - every guide shows its last-updated month so you can judge freshness

How we make money (and our promise)

Links marked as booking options take you to AAL Fast Track (fasttrack.aal.vn), a Vietnamese airport-assistance company. If you book through our link, AAL pays us a referral commission. You pay exactly the same price as booking directly - the commission comes out of AAL's margin, not your pocket.

That relationship is why this site is free to read. It does not decide what we write: when fast track is not worth it, we say so, and our wait-time guides are written so you can judge for yourself whether you need it.

Corrections and contact

Airport procedures change. If you spot something out of date - a price, a desk location, a rule - tell us and we will fix it. See how to reach us.
