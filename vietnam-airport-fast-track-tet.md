# Vietnam Airport Fast Track During Tet (Lunar New Year)


## What Tet does to Vietnam's airports

Tet Nguyen Dan (Lunar New Year) is the largest human migration in Vietnam's calendar. In 2027, Tet falls on6 February, with the heaviest travel running roughly 30 January - 10 February. The airport picture splits in two:

- Domestic terminals explode- Tan Son Nhat domestic is famously chaotic in the days before Tet, with check-in and security lines spilling outside. Noi Bai domestic is similar.
- International arrivals rise steadily- overseas Vietnamese (Viet Kieu) arriving for Tet add load to immigration halls in the 7-10 days before the holiday, especially on flights from the US, Australia and Europe.

## Where it actually hurts

- International-to-domestic connections- the worst single scenario: landing at SGN international then fighting the domestic terminal for a flight to Da Nang or Hue during Tet week. An escorted transfer across terminals saves real time and stress.
- Departure day- leaving Vietnam right after Tet means crowded check-in, long security, and packed exit-immigration. A departure escort pays for itself most clearly on these days.
- Ground transport on Tet Eve- taxis and Grab thin out drastically on the afternoon of Tet Eve and the first morning. Pre-booked pickup or a meet-and-greet with transfer avoids being stranded.

## Planning checklist for a Tet arrival

- Book fast track 2-4 weeks out- holiday slots are the first to fill because each booking needs a named agent on your specific flight.
- Have your e-visa done before departure- visa-on-arrival queues add a full second wait during the Tet rush; see thee-visa processing time guide.
- Leave buffer for domestic connections- treat anything under 2.5 hours as risky during Tet week without an escorted transfer.
- Pre-arrange the last mile- hotel pickup or a driver beats the taxi queue, especially on Tet Eve and the first two days.
