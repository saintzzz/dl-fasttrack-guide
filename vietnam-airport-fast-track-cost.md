## Typical price ranges

| Service level | Typical range (per person) | What it buys |

| Standard priority arrival | ~US$20-50 | Gate meet, priority immigration lane, escort to baggage and exit |

| VIP arrival / departure | ~US$40-80+ | Above + lounge access, buggy transport, full luggage handling |

| Departure escort | ~US$20-50 | Check-in help, priority security + exit immigration |

| Transit escort | ~US$30-60 | Meet at arrival gate, guided transfer to onward flight |

Prices float with season, group size and lead time - treat these as orientation, then check live pricing for your specific date and airport.

## What moves the price

- Airport. HCMC (SGN) and Hanoi (HAN) have the deepest agent supply; smaller airports can price differently for the same service level.

- Tier. The price gap between standard and VIP is mostly lounge access and luggage handling - the immigration skip itself is the same.

- Group size. Per-person pricing usually means a family of four pays roughly 4x - though child discounts are common.

- Lead time. Booking days ahead is cheaper than arranging at the airport, and guarantees an agent is rostered to your flight.

- Visa-on-arrival handling. Packages that include Landing Visa counter paperwork may sit at the top of the range - the agent is doing more work.

## Is it worth the money?

The honest math: at peak arrival banks you are buying 30-75 minutes of your first day in Vietnam for the price of a nice dinner. After a 12-hour flight, with kids, elderly travelers, or a tight domestic connection, that trade is easy to justify. On a quiet midday arrival in low season it is harder - read the full worth-it breakdown for the skip-it cases.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does fast track cost at Vietnam airports?** — Roughly US$20-50 per person for standard arrival priority, US$40-80+ for VIP tiers with lounge and luggage extras. Live pricing varies by airport, tier, group size and date.

**Per person or per group?** — Per person, almost always. Children often have discounts - check the per-head breakdown on the booking page.

**Standard vs VIP - what changes?** — The immigration skip is identical. VIP adds lounge access while paperwork is handled, buggy transport and hands-off luggage service.

**Cheaper to book in advance?** — Yes - advance online booking beats walk-up pricing and guarantees an agent on your flight.
