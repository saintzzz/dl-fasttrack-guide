## Email

Write to us at linhld@vieschool.com . We are a small team in Hanoi (GMT+7) and typically reply within 1-2 business days.

Email the guide team

## What to write about

- Corrections. A price, desk, rule or wait time that has changed - the fastest way to get it fixed for the next traveler.

- Arrival reports. Your own timing at HAN, SGN or DAD helps us keep the wait-time guides honest.

- Content questions. Anything unclear in a guide - if it confused you, it will confuse others.

## Booking support

VieSchool Fast Track is a travel guide, not the service provider. For anything about an existing booking - schedule changes, meeting points, refunds - contact AAL Fast Track directly; they handle all service operations.

## Visa and immigration

For official visa status, use the government's e-visa portal and your application code. We publish guides but cannot check or influence individual applications.

## What to include in your message

- Corrections: the page URL, the outdated detail, and a source we can check (provider page, official tariff, your dated receipt). We re-verify against the provider before changing numbers.

- Booking support: your arrival airport, flight number and date - we route it to the operator team who can actually see your booking.

- Guide requests: airports or arrival situations not covered yet - transit between terminals, land-border crossings, group sizes above 10.

We answer within two working days. For anything time-critical on the day of travel, contact the operator directly - their confirmation email includes the on-day WhatsApp line.
