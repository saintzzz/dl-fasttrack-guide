Is Vietnam airport fast track worth it? An honest verdict by airport.

Updated for the 2026 travel season - no hype, just when it pays and when it doesn't.

The short answer

Fast track is worth the money at SGN almost any evening, at HAN during peak flight banks, and at every airport during Tet and public holidays. It is also worth it regardless of queues if you travel with small children, elderly parents, a visa-on-arrival approval letter, or a tight onward connection. On a quiet midday arrival at Da Nang with an e-visa already printed - you can probably skip it.

Who benefits most

  • Families with small children - an hour in a hot queue after a long flight is when meltdowns happen. The agent walks you past it.
  • Elderly travelers - standing 60+ minutes is genuinely hard; priority lane is a health consideration, not a luxury.
  • Visa-on-arrival holders - the VOA desk is a second queue after the first one. The agent does the stamping for you while you sit.
  • Tight connections - domestic flight, cruise departure, Ha Long Bay transfer: 30 saved minutes can be the difference.
  • Late-night arrivals - after 22:00 the queue still forms, and your hotel bed is calling.

When you can skip it

  • Midday weekday arrivals at Da Nang (DAD) - often through in 15-30 minutes.
  • Off-season quiet banks at HAN (check your landing time against peak banks in our wait times guide).
  • You have an e-visa printed, hand luggage only, and no fixed onward plan - worst case you wait 30-40 minutes.

The cost math

A typical fast track booking costs about the same as an airport coffee-and-meal for two - while the queue it skips is usually the longest, hottest, most crowded hour of your whole trip. If fast track saves you even 40 minutes of standing after a 10-hour flight, most travelers consider that the best money spent on the trip. The honest counter-argument: if you land at a genuinely quiet window, it is money you didn't need to spend - which is why we publish the hour-by-hour wait data so you can judge your own flight.

How fast track actually works

You book online before you fly. An agent meets you at the arrival gate holding a name board, handles any visa paperwork if needed, and escorts you through the priority immigration lane. Gate to arrivals hall: roughly 10-15 minutes instead of 45-100. Full step-by-step flow in our e-visa + fast track guide.
