Priority - the right answer for most people

Gate meet, priority lane, guidance to baggage. This is the entire reason fast track exists: the 60-100 minute immigration queue becomes ~15 minutes. Everything above this tier is comfort, not speed.

Book this if: you have an e-visa printed, can handle your own bags, and just want out of the airport fast.

Business Class - the middle rung

Adds name-board greeting, visa-on-arrival assistance, and escort through to transport - and on departure, priority check-in counters plus priority security. Named for what it is: the business-class ground experience without the business-class ticket.

Book this if: you are on a VoA approval letter (the visa desk alone adds 20-40 minutes), or departing during check-in rush.

VIP - everything handled

The full-service wrap: name board, visa assistance, priority lane, carousel baggage collection, customs declaration support, lost-luggage handling, escort out to your pickup or arranged transport. You essentially stop doing airport chores entirely.

Book this if: elderly parents, young kids, lots of luggage, first trip to Vietnam, or you simply want zero decisions after a long flight.

VIP B - the no-stop tier

The top product in the market's vocabulary: staff take your passport and complete immigration at the Special Priority lane while you are walked through without stopping - no booth queue, no document check at all. Passport comes back minutes later. The closest thing to a diplomatic channel.

Book this if: executives on a clock, travelers who physically cannot stand at a counter, or anyone for whom even 15 minutes is too long. Honest note: the time saving over regular Priority is ~10 minutes - you are paying for effortlessness, not just speed.

Departure and transit tiers

Departures run the same ladder flipped: Priority = check-in assist + priority exit-immigration + security. VIP B departure adds priority check-in counters, passport-handled exit formalities, and escort to the aircraft door. Transit escorts are a separate package combining arrival + terminal transfer + re-check-in.

Frequently asked questions

Priority vs VIP difference?

Same lane, same skip. VIP adds name board, visa assist, baggage, customs, lost-luggage and curb escort.

What is VIP B?

Staff do your immigration at the Special Priority lane while you walk through without stopping - passport returned after.

Business Class vs VIP?

Business Class = priority counters + security (departure strength). VIP = arrival-side full service: bags, customs, escort.

Which tier should I book?

Priority for most; VIP for VoA paperwork/elderly/heavy bags/first-timers; VIP B for executives and cannot-stand cases.
