Vietnam Airport Meet and Greet: How the Service Works

"Meet and greet" sounds vague until you land jetlagged at a crowded terminal. In Vietnam it means a named agent holding a board with your name at the aircraft door, who then walks you past every queue. Here is exactly what happens, step by step.

Last updated November 2026 · Available at Noi Bai (HAN), Tan Son Nhat (SGN), Da Nang (DAD)

Arrival service, step by step

  1. The meet. An agent waits at the arrival gate or aerobridge exit holding a board with your name. On remote-stand arrivals they meet you at the terminal entrance bus doors instead.
  2. Visa paperwork (if needed). On a visa-on-arrival letter, the agent takes your documents to the Landing Visa counter and handles the exchange while you wait a few steps away.
  3. Priority immigration. You are escorted to the crew/diplomat lane - typically a 2-5 minute process versus 45-90 minutes in the main queue at peak.
  4. Baggage and out. The agent walks you to the belt, helps with bags (VIP tiers handle this fully), and escorts you through customs to the arrivals hall - where your driver, Grab or SIM counter is.

Departure and transit, briefly

  • Departure: the agent meets you at the terminal entrance, assists at check-in, escorts you through priority security and exit immigration, and leaves you at the gate or lounge. Useful on the morning departure peaks and for travelers who want zero navigation stress.
  • Transit: for international-to-domestic connections the agent bridges the terminal change (T2→T1 at Hanoi, separate domestic terminal at HCMC) and makes a tight connection survivable.

What "VIP" adds over standard

  • Lounge access while any paperwork is processed
  • Buggy transport through long terminal stretches
  • Full luggage handling from belt to your car

The core value - the priority lane - is identical between standard and VIP. Choose VIP when the extras themselves are the point, not the queue.

What you need to provide at booking

  • Flight number and date - the agent roster keys off this, and they track delays.
  • Passenger names as per passport - they go on the board and the service manifest.
  • Visa status - e-visa, VOA letter, or exempt - so the agent knows whether the Landing Visa counter is on your route.
  • A reachable contact - WhatsApp/Zalo or a local SIM once landed.

Frequently asked questions

What does the service include?

Agent at the gate with a name board, priority immigration escort, VOA paperwork handling if needed, and a walk to baggage and the exit. Departure and transit versions mirror it.

How do I find the agent?

At the arrival gate or aerobridge exit with your name on a board - before you merge into the terminal flow. The booking confirmation has the exact meeting point and a hotline.

Is meet and greet the same as fast track?

Mostly, in Vietnam. 'Meet and greet' = the personal escort; 'fast track' = the priority lane they use. Most packages include both.

Can I book it for departure?

Yes - the agent meets you at the terminal, helps check in, and escorts you through priority security and exit immigration to the gate or lounge.
