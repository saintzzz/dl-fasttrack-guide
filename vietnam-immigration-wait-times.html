How Long Is Immigration at Vietnam Airports?

Immigration is the longest part of arriving in Vietnam. Wait times vary a lot by airport, time of day and season - this guide covers real numbers at each major airport, what causes the queues, and how to get through faster.

Noi Bai (HAN), Hanoi - 45 to 90 minutes at peak

Terminal 2 handles all international arrivals. The terminal is modern and reasonably organized, but staffing at immigration counters does not scale with the flight schedule. The worst queues form in two predictable banks:

  • 5-8am - the European arrivals wave: Qatar, Emirates, Turkish and Vietnam Airlines' overnight flights from Paris, London and Frankfurt land within a short window, putting 1,000+ passengers into the hall at once.
  • 7-10pm - the East Asia evening bank: flights from Seoul, Tokyo, Taipei, Hong Kong and mainland China arrive together.

Off-peak midday arrivals can be through in 20-30 minutes. Vietnam has few e-gates for foreign passports, so almost everyone queues at staffed counters - the queue is the queue.

Tan Son Nhat (SGN), Ho Chi Minh City - the longest queues in Vietnam

SGN consistently has the country's worst immigration waits. 60-100 minutes is common in the evening and at weekends, and Tet plus public holidays can push it well beyond that. Three things compound the problem:

  • The international terminal handles more passengers than it was designed for.
  • Several long-haul flights from Europe, Australia and the Middle East land between 6pm and 11pm.
  • The visa-on-arrival desk adds a second queue inside the same hall for anyone on an approval letter.

If you arrive 6pm-11pm or on a weekend, budget real time for the queue - or use a priority lane.

Da Nang (DAD) - shorter but still spiky

Da Nang is smaller and usually faster: 20-40 minutes is normal and quiet arrivals can be faster still. The airport still spikes during Tet, summer holidays (June-August) and when several charter flights land together - especially Chinese and Korean tour groups, which arrive in large blocks with group paperwork that slows the counters.

What the arrival flow actually looks like

  1. Gate to immigration hall - a 3-10 minute walk, sometimes with an escalator queue forming before the hall.
  2. Visa counter (VoA only) - travelers on visa-on-arrival approval letters stop at the Landing Visa desk first. This is a separate queue inside the same hall and can add 20-40 minutes at peak.
  3. Immigration counters - the main queue. Officers check passport, visa and take a photo. 20-100 minutes depending on the bank.
  4. Baggage claim - usually fast, 10-20 minutes, because immigration delays mean bags are already on the belt.
  5. Customs and arrivals hall - walk through unless flagged.

What actually makes the queue faster

  • Have papers ready - passport open, e-visa printout or approval letter in hand. Every fumble adds 30 seconds per person ahead of you.
  • Fill the arrival form on the plane if your airline hands it out.
  • Pick the right line - counters at the edges of the hall often move faster than the center where most people drift.
  • Priority lanes - families with infants and diplomats can sometimes use them, but they are not reliably open to tourists without a fast track booking.
  • Fast track meet & greet - the reliable way to skip the queue entirely. An agent meets you at the gate and escorts you through the priority lane - typically 10-15 minutes gate to exit.

When fast track is worth the money

It pays off at SGN almost any evening, at HAN during peak banks, and everywhere during Tet and public holidays. It is also worth it with elderly travelers, small children, or tight onward connections - a 90-minute queue can kill a domestic connection or a train you planned to catch. On a quiet midday Da Nang arrival you can probably skip it.

Frequently asked questions

How long is immigration at Tan Son Nhat airport?

Expect 60-100 minutes during evening peaks (6pm-11pm) and weekends. Off-peak midday arrivals can clear in 30-45 minutes. SGN consistently has the longest immigration queues in Vietnam.

How long is immigration at Noi Bai airport?

Noi Bai averages 45-90 minutes at peak: early morning (5-8am) when European flights land together, and the evening East Asia bank (7-10pm). Midday arrivals can be through in 20-30 minutes.

Is fast track at Vietnam airports worth it?

Fast track pays off at SGN almost any evening, at HAN during peak flight banks, and everywhere during Tet and public holidays. It is also worth it with elderly travelers, small children, or tight onward connections.

How does fast track work at Vietnam airports?

An agent meets you at the arrival gate holding a name board, handles any visa paperwork if needed, and escorts you through the priority immigration lane. The whole process from gate to arrivals hall takes about 10-15 minutes.

What is the busiest time at Vietnam airports?

The worst windows are early morning (5-8am) when European flights land, evenings (6-11pm) when regional flights bunch together, weekends, and Tet holiday plus summer peak season (June-August).

Do I need to fill in an arrival form for Vietnam?

Some airlines hand out arrival forms on the plane - fill it in before landing to save time at the counter. Have your passport and e-visa printout or approval letter ready. Fast track agents handle the paperwork for you.
