Where Newway SAS wins

  • Coverage nobody else matches - the only provider listing all five airports including Cam Ranh for Nha Trang.
  • Lowest entry price published - 405k VND undercuts even Heera's $28 (~700k VND) at the bottom tier.
  • Booking code instantly - your reference appears as soon as the form goes in; lookup works with code + email/phone.
  • Free cancellation to 24h - more flexible than most prepaid checkouts.

Where it loses ground

  • Callback dependency - the booking is not confirmed until operations phones you. Advertised 15 minutes, but it is still a human loop - awkward if you are in a different timezone or on a plane already.
  • Payment handled at confirmation call - not an instant card checkout; terms get settled verbally.
  • Thin English web presence - fewer reviews and English-language depth than the bigger D2C sites.

The verdict

For Cam Ranh and Phu Quoc Newway SAS is often the only serious option - take it. For SGN, HAN and DAD it competes on price, but you trade instant confirmation for a phone call. If you want the booking finished before you close the laptop - live price, card, confirmation - a direct checkout is the shorter path. Compare all eight providers.

Frequently asked questions

Is Newway SAS legit?

Yes - real operator, widest published coverage (5 airports), transparent VND pricing, 15-min callback promise, free cancel to 24h.

How much?

405,000-3,000,000 VND per guest depending on package and airport.

How do I book?

Six-step form → instant booking code → operations calls you to confirm timing and payment.

Newway vs AAL?

CXr/PQC: Newway only. SGN/HAN/DAD: Newway cheaper entry but callback model; AAL instant checkout + card + 2h SLA.

Why is New Way SAS so much cheaper?

Local-market pricing - they sell primarily to Vietnamese travelers in VND through Zalo, without an English-language booking operation to maintain. The escort itself is the same idea; you are not paying for English support, polished confirmation emails or a self-serve checkout.

Can I book New Way in English?

WhatsApp works, and basic English coordination is usually fine - but instructions, signboards and agent communication run Vietnamese-first. For a first-time arrival juggling visa and SIM questions, an English-native service is less stressful.
