Vietnam Arrival Card: What You Actually Fill Out

Search results on this are a mess of outdated advice. The short version: the classic paper arrival card is mostly dead, there is no mandatory online pre-arrival form, and what you actually need at the desk is a short document list. Here is the honest picture.

Last updated November 2026 · Applies at all Vietnam international airports

Is the paper arrival card still required?

For most travelers, no. Vietnam phased out the mandatory arrival/departure card years ago for visitors holding visas or visa exemptions - immigration records your entry electronically. Some airlines still distribute the old form onboard out of habit; fill it if handed one, but it is rarely collected and missing it is not a problem.

Is there an online arrival form?

No mandatory one. For e-visa travelers the online application IS the advance paperwork - once issued, nothing further needs to be submitted online. The Covid-era health-declaration requirement ended in 2022; any guide still telling you to fill a health form is outdated.

What each traveler type actually needs

Entry typeWhat you show at the desk
E-visa holderPassport + printed e-visa
Visa-exempt nationalPassport only (check your country's exempt days)
Visa-on-arrival letterPassport + approval letter + NA1 form (filled at the Landing Visa desk)

Only VoA travelers fill a real form on arrival - the NA1 application handed out at the Landing Visa counter. Your agent or the fast track desk can pre-fill it.

Side paperwork that still exists

  • Customs declaration - needed only if you carry over US$5,000 cash equivalent, more than 350 cigarettes, over 1.5L of spirits, or dutiable goods. A red-channel form, not for everyone.
  • Hotel address - not a form, but officers occasionally ask where you stay first. Have the name ready on your phone.
  • Onward ticket - airlines sometimes check at departure more than immigration does on arrival; a return or onward booking is good practice.

The paperwork order of operations

  1. Weeks before: apply for the e-visa (or buy a VoA approval letter if you must use VoA).
  2. On the plane: fill whatever form the crew hands you - usually nothing, occasionally the legacy card or a customs slip.
  3. At the desk: passport + printed e-visa (or approval letter, at the Landing Visa counter first).
  4. After immigration: baggage claim, then the green customs channel unless you have declarable goods.

Paperwork done, the only remaining variable is the queue itself - see how long immigration takes at your arrival airport, and the meet and greet service if you want an agent handling the whole desk sequence.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an arrival card for Vietnam?

Most travelers no - the mandatory paper card was phased out. Fill whatever the cabin crew hands you, but it is rarely collected.

What form do I fill on the plane?

Usually nothing, or the legacy arrival/departure card or a customs declaration if the crew distributes one.

Is there an online arrival form before flying?

No mandatory one. The e-visa application is the advance paperwork; the health declaration ended in 2022.

What do I show at immigration?

Passport (6+ months), printed e-visa or VoA approval letter, sometimes your first hotel address. Cash declarations apply above US$5,000.
