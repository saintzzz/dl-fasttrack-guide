## Where Heera wins

- Daytime price floor - $28 undercuts everyone publishing numbers: AVIA $39, AAL $43, FastTrackVNS/VJP $35.

- Transparent packages - real prices on the page, unusual in a market full of "send us your flight for a quote."

- Honest fine print - they publish the night surcharge and caveat that no exact processing time is guaranteed.

## Where it loses ground

- Two airports only - Da Nang and Phu Quoc are "contact us" inventory, not bookable standard.

- Night surcharge eats the edge - a 23:00-06:00 arrival adds $6, landing at $34 - within $5-9 of rivals with no night fee. SGN's worst queue window is evening arrivals.

- No instant checkout SLA - form plus advice means confirmation timing is conversational, not contractual like AAL's 2 working hours.

## The verdict

For a solo traveler landing at SGN or HAN in daytime, Heera's $28 is the floor and genuinely hard to beat. For families the math still favors it (~$60 saved across four people). But compare the final number for your flight: night arrivals, tight timelines or Da Nang/Phu Quoc erase the advantage - and for a $15 difference, a guaranteed instant confirmation has real value at 11pm before a 7am flight.
