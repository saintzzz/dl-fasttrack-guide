# Vietnam Airport Fast Track at Christmas & New Year


## Why the last two weeks of December are different

Late December concentrates three demand waves into Vietnam's airports: foreign tourists on Christmas and New Year holidays, overseas Vietnamese flying home early for Tet planning, and Koreans and Japanese on winter breaks. At Tan Son Nhat the worst windows are typically 11:00-14:00 and 19:00-23:00, when wide-bodies arrive in clusters and every immigration booth backs up.

New Year week repeats the pattern in reverse - heavy departures in the first days of January, when exit-immigration and security queues at SGN stretch just as long as the arrival lines did a week earlier.


## What it means for your arrival

- Longer immigration queues- 45-70 minute waits are realistic at peak on Dec 24-26 and Dec 31, versus a 20-40 minute normal-peak wait.
- Slower baggage and ground transport- taxi and Grab lines at SGN lengthen in step; families with luggage feel it most.
- Tighter connections- domestic onward flights to Phu Quoc, Nha Trang and Da Lat sell out; a 90-minute layover gets risky without assistance.

## How to arrive smart during the holidays

- Sort the visa before you fly- e-visa holders go straight to immigration; visa-on-arrival desks add a second queue on busy days. See oure-visa fast track guide.
- Book fast track early- holiday slots are limited because each booking needs a named agent on your flight; last-minute requests on Dec 24-26 are the ones most likely to be declined.
- Land outside peak banks where possible- early-morning and late-night arrivals clear noticeably faster than the midday and evening waves.
- Pre-arrange onward transport- post-holiday taxi queues at SGN can run 30+ minutes; a driver waiting with a name sign removes that leg entirely.

## Cost during the holiday period

Per-person pricing stays in the usual ranges - roughly US$20-50 for standard priority arrival and US$40-80+ for VIP tiers - but some providers add holiday surcharges on Dec 24-25 and Dec 31-Jan 1. The constraint is availability more than price: holiday weeks are when advance booking stops being optional.
