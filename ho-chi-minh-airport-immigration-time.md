| Late night (after midnight) | 20-35 min |

| Fast track, any window | ~10-15 min |

SGN is the one Vietnam airport where "how bad can it be" has a real answer: the evening bank regularly produces the country's longest queues.

## Why SGN's peak hours are so bad

- Volume over design - Terminal 2 handles far more passengers than it was built for; counter staffing never caught up.

- The 6-11pm long-haul wave - Europe, Australia, the Middle East and Northeast Asia all arrive in the same evening window.

- A queue inside the queue - visa-on-arrival letter holders stop at the Landing Visa counter first, adding 20-40 minutes before the main line.

- Tet and summer peak - around Tet and June-August, 100-minute waits are common rather than exceptional.

## When you can get lucky

- Midday midweek arrivals - the genuine quiet window; 30-45 minutes is realistic.

- Very late night - after midnight the hall thins out, though you trade queue time for a very late arrival into the city.

- Low season weekdays - outside Tet and summer, off-peak waits can drop below 30 minutes.

## Connecting onward? The queue decides

Domestic flights leave from Terminal 1 or the new Terminal 3 - a 5-15 minute landside transfer. That transfer is easy; the unpredictable part is immigration. A 90-minute queue against a 2-hour domestic connection is a missed flight. If you are connecting to Phu Quoc, Da Lat or the Mekong region on a separate ticket, the immigration wait is your real risk - read the SGN fast track guide before relying on the connection.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long is immigration at Ho Chi Minh City airport?** — 60-100+ minutes in the 6-11pm evening peak, 30-45 minutes at quiet midday hours, and up to 2 hours on bad holiday evenings. VoA letter holders queue twice.

**What are the peak hours at Tan Son Nhat?** — 6-11pm is the main peak - the long-haul arrival wave. Weekends, Tet and summer make it worse. Midday midweek is the calmest.

**Is Saigon airport the same as Tan Son Nhat?** — Yes - same airport, code SGN. International arrivals use Terminal 2.

**How do I skip the queue?** — Fast track - agent at the T2 gate, priority immigration lane, ~10-15 minutes gate to curb. No free tourist priority lane exists at SGN.
