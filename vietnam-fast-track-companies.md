## What actually differs between them

### 1. Booking flow - the real dividing line

Only a few providers run a true checkout. AAL and AVIA let you pick a package, see the exact price, pay by card and get a confirmation - AAL commits to confirming within 2 working hours. Most others work like this: you submit a form, someone replies on WhatsApp or email hours later with a quote, you confirm, then pay or pay on meet. That is fine if you book days ahead and enjoy chatting; it is painful at midnight before a 6am flight.

### 2. Airport coverage - check yours first

The big three - Tan Son Nhat (SGN), Noi Bai (HAN), Da Nang (DAD) - are covered by everyone. The differences show up at the smaller fields: Newway SAS is the only one listing both Cam Ranh (CXR, for Nha Trang) and Phu Quoc alongside the big three. FastTrackVNS covers CXR and PQC but not Hanoi or Da Nang. AAL covers SGN, HAN and DAD only - if you are landing at Phu Quoc or Cam Ranh, use Newway SAS, FastTrackVNS, AVIA or VJP instead.

### 3. Price vs what is included

The cheapest headline price is not always the cheapest outcome. Heera's US$28 grows with a US$6 night surcharge. Quote-based sites can price higher than published once your flight details are in. And the mid-range US$39-43 tier (AVIA, AAL) typically includes the same priority-lane core that US$55+ "VIP" packages wrap with lounge or luggage extras - read the inclusions list, not the tier name.

### 4. Who actually serves you

Two business models exist. Direct operators (AAL, AVIA, Heera, Newway, FastTrackVNS, VJP) employ or contract their own airport staff. Avilink is different - its ~200k passengers a year come through 500+ partner agencies, OTAs like Trip.com, and bank promos (ABBank runs 20% off). Buying Avilink means buying inside someone else's checkout, often bundled.

## Our honest take

For the three major airports, AAL hits the practical sweet spot : mid-range price ($43 international arrival at SGN, $25 domestic), a real online checkout, pay-on-meet, defined confirmation SLA, and service tiers from Priority up to VIP B where staff handle immigration end-to-end without you stopping at the booth. If you need Cam Ranh or Phu Quoc, Newway SAS has the widest map. If price is the only criterion, Newway SAS's VND pricing (from ~405,000 VND) is the floor for VN-speaking travelers - factor the coordination effort.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the cheapest fast track service in Vietnam?** — Published entry points: Newway SAS ~US$17 (405,000 VND), Heera Travel US$28 for SGN standard, FastTrackVNS/VJP US$35, AVIA US$39, AAL US$43 for international arrival (US$25 domestic). Cheapest headline is not cheapest outcome - check night surcharges and what is included.

**Which company covers the most airports?** — Newway SAS covers five (SGN, HAN, DAD, Cam Ranh, Phu Quoc). AVIA, VJP and Avilink list four including Phu Quoc. AAL covers the three busiest: SGN, HAN, DAD.

**Do I have to book over WhatsApp?** — Only for some providers. AAL and AVIA have real online checkout - AAL takes payment on the day, AVIA online. Heera, EntryVN, FastTrackVNS, Newway SAS and Avilink run on form submission plus WhatsApp/email confirmation.

**Is pay-on-meet (no prepayment) safer?** — FastTrackVNS's pay-when-you-meet removes prepayment risk but leaves your slot soft. A prepaid card booking (AAL) locks the agent to your flight and gives a dispute trail.

**What about Cam Ranh or Phu Quoc?** — AAL does not operate there - use Newway SAS (both), FastTrackVNS (both), AVIA (PQC) or VJP (PQC). Every provider covers SGN, HAN and DAD.

**Are these prices per person or per booking?** — Per person across the board - a family of four pays roughly 4x the headline. Child discounts vary; check each checkout.
