Pricing is strictly per person - there is no flat family rate. The honest trade: four people × $43 buys back the same 60-100 minutes for everyone at once, and removes the hardest version of the queue (the one with kids and grandparents in it).

## What the agent actually does for a group

- One name sign, one meeting point - the whole party is collected at the gate together; nobody navigates alone.

- All passports handled as one batch - the agent carries the group's documents through the priority booth, so kids and seniors never face a counter alone.

- Keeps everyone together - no splitting across different queue lines, no "wait here while dad queues."

- Baggage + customs wrangling - VIP tiers cover carousel and customs, meaning one adult is not juggling trolleys and toddlers.

- Handover at the curb - elderly parents traveling alone get escorted out and handed to whoever is picking them up, with the pickup arranged in advance.

## When it is clearly worth it

- Any evening arrival at SGN with kids - the 6-11pm wave is 60-100 minutes of standing; children do not last that queue.

- Elderly parents, any airport - standing time is a health issue, not a comfort issue.

- Groups of 6+ - the logistics tax scales with headcount; one agent holding the group together is worth more per person, not less.

- Visa-on-arrival parties - a family on VoA letters queues twice; the agent handles the landing-visa desk for everyone at once.

## When a group can skip it

- Midday Da Nang arrival, adults only, carry-on - a 20-minute queue is survivable.

- Budget groups where $40×8 genuinely strains the trip - in that case pick the package for the grandparents only and let the adults queue (agents can escort a partial party).
