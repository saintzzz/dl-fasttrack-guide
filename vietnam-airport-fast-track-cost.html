Vietnam Airport Fast Track Cost: What You'll Actually Pay

Fast track at Vietnamese airports is a per-person service, and the price depends on three things: which airport, which service tier, and how far ahead you book. Here is how the pricing works and what each tier actually buys.

What moves the price

  • Airport. HCMC (SGN) and Hanoi (HAN) have the deepest agent supply; smaller airports can price differently for the same service level.
  • Tier. The price gap between standard and VIP is mostly lounge access and luggage handling - the immigration skip itself is the same.
  • Group size. Per-person pricing usually means a family of four pays roughly 4x - though child discounts are common.
  • Lead time. Booking days ahead is cheaper than arranging at the airport, and guarantees an agent is rostered to your flight.
  • Visa-on-arrival handling. Packages that include Landing Visa counter paperwork may sit at the top of the range - the agent is doing more work.

Is it worth the money?

The honest math: at peak arrival banks you are buying 30-75 minutes of your first day in Vietnam for the price of a nice dinner. After a 12-hour flight, with kids, elderly travelers, or a tight domestic connection, that trade is easy to justify. On a quiet midday arrival in low season it is harder - read the full worth-it breakdown for the skip-it cases.

How to book

  1. Pick your airport and service tier on the booking page.
  2. Enter flight number and arrival date - the agent assignment keys off this.
  3. Pay online, receive confirmation with the meeting instructions.
  4. Land, look for your name on the board, walk past the queue.

Frequently asked questions

How much does fast track cost at Vietnam airports?

Roughly US$20-50 per person for standard arrival priority, US$40-80+ for VIP tiers with lounge and luggage extras. Live pricing varies by airport, tier, group size and date.

Per person or per group?

Per person, almost always. Children often have discounts - check the per-head breakdown on the booking page.

Standard vs VIP - what changes?

The immigration skip is identical. VIP adds lounge access while paperwork is handled, buggy transport and hands-off luggage service.

Cheaper to book in advance?

Yes - advance online booking beats walk-up pricing and guarantees an agent on your flight.
