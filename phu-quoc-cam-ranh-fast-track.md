## What to expect at these airports

- Smaller but spiky queues. PQC and CXR handle fewer flights, but a single widebody charter can flood the small immigration hall - waits jump from 10 minutes to 45+ fast.

- Thin agent supply. Fewer staff are rostered than at SGN/HAN - book early for peak season (December-February, summer holidays) or you may find no availability at all.

- Domestic arrivals need nothing. If you fly Hanoi or HCMC → PQC on a domestic leg, you walk straight out - no immigration at all.

## The transit-through-SGN/HAN play

Many Phu Quoc and Nha Trang itineraries actually enter Vietnam at SGN or HAN , then connect to a domestic flight. In that case the fast track decision splits in two:

- International entry (SGN/HAN): the real queue. Book priority arrival here - AAL covers both airports with instant checkout and a 2-hour confirmation SLA.

- Domestic leg to PQC/CXR: no immigration, walk out - nothing needed.

If the connection is tight, an AAL transit escort handles the whole chain: priority entry immigration, bags, terminal transfer, priority re-check-in. That is often worth more than arrival-only fast track at the destination airport.

**Land at SGN/HAN first?** — Book fast track at the international entry (AAL covers SGN/HAN), the domestic leg needs nothing. Tight connection? Use a transit escort.
