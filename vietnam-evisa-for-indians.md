# Vietnam E-Visa for Indians: Fees, Steps and Processing Time

## Eligibility - yes, Indians qualify

Vietnam's e-visa program opened to all nationalities in August 2023. Indian passport holders are fully eligible - this is now the recommended route, replacing the old embassy-application path. You need a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date.

## Fees - the official numbers only

| Type | Fee | Validity |

| Single entry | US$25 | Up to 90 days |

| Multiple entry | US$50 | Up to 90 days |

Pay by card on the official portal (evisa.gov.vn). Lookalike agent sites rank on Google too and add service fees of US$20-60 for the same visa - check the URL before paying.

## Application, step by step

- Prepare documents - a scan of your passport data page and a passport-style photo (white background, no glasses, recent).

- Fill the form on evisa.gov.vn - personal details must match the passport exactly. Indian names cause the most stalls here: given-name/surname order matters, and a surname left blank on the passport needs careful handling.

- Declare entry and exit ports - pick the actual airport you land at (Noi Bai, Tan Son Nhat, Da Nang...). The visa is tied to this port.

- Pay and save the registration code - you will need it to check status and download the visa PDF.

- Print the e-visa - carry a paper copy; airlines and immigration both ask for it.

## Processing time - official vs real

Officially 3 working days. Realistically 3-10 days - the two common stalls are photo rejection and name-order mismatches, both of which send the application back to the end of the queue. Apply at least 2 weeks out; the portal has no real urgent lane. Details in the processing-time deep dive .

## E-visa vs visa on arrival for Indians

Both are technically available, but VoA is the worse path for Indians now: you must buy an approval letter from an agent first (extra fee, extra wait), then queue at the Landing Visa counter on arrival before joining the main immigration queue - two queues instead of one. E-visa holders walk straight to immigration. Full comparison in visa on arrival vs e-visa .

## When you land - the part nobody warns you about

Your e-visa gets you permission to board and to enter - it does not get you through the airport fast. On a peak-window arrival at Tan Son Nhat or Noi Bai, the immigration queue after a 4-5 hour flight from India can run 60-100 minutes. If you are landing in the evening or traveling with family, arrival fast track is the difference between a long queue and a 10-15 minute exit.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can Indian citizens get a Vietnam e-visa?** — Yes - fully eligible since August 2023. It is the standard recommended route; no embassy visit needed.

**How much does it cost?** — US$25 single-entry, US$50 multiple-entry on the official portal. Third-party sites add service fees for the same visa.

**How many days does processing take?** — 3 working days officially, 3-10 in practice - photo rejections and name-order issues push applications back into the queue. Apply 2+ weeks ahead.

**How long is it valid?** — Up to 90 days from your declared entry date; single or multiple entry by fee tier. Tied to the arrival port you declare.

**Is visa on arrival better for Indians?** — No - VoA needs a paid approval letter first and adds a second airport queue. E-visa is simpler end to end.
