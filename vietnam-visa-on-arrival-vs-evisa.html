Vietnam Visa on Arrival vs E-Visa: Which One to Get

Two legal ways to get a Vietnam visa before you fly - and they are not equal. The e-visa wins on price and paperwork; the approval letter still makes sense in specific cases. Here is the honest comparison, including the airport wait nobody mentions.

When the e-visa is the right call

  • You have a week or more before departure. Three working days plus buffer is comfortable; the fee is lower and there is no cash paperwork at the airport.
  • You want the simplest arrival. E-visa holders skip the Landing Visa counter entirely and join the same immigration queue as visa-exempt travelers.
  • You are crossing by land or sea. E-visas cover specific land borders and seaports that visa-on-arrival letters do not.

When the VOA letter still wins

  • Very urgent trips. Letter services offer same-day and even few-hour turnaround for a premium - faster than the e-visa can go.
  • A failed e-visa application close to departure. If the e-visa portal rejected or stalled your application days before the flight, an express letter is the reliable fallback.
  • Preference for a full-page visa sticker - relevant for travelers who want it for onward paperwork.

The arrival-time difference (the part people miss)

Whichever route you choose, you still face the main immigration queue - 45-90 minutes at peak banks. The difference is one extra stop:

  1. E-visa: plane → immigration queue → bags → out. One queue.
  2. VOA letter: plane → Landing Visa counter (exchange letter for sticker) → immigration queue → bags → out. Two queues.

This is exactly the scenario where a meet-and-greet service earns its fee: the agent handles the Landing Visa counter paperwork while you wait nearby, then escorts you through the priority lane - turning what can be a two-hour arrival into about 15-20 minutes.

Common mistakes both ways

  • Wrong entry port on the e-visa. The visa names a specific port; landing elsewhere can mean refusal at the counter.
  • VOA without cash. The stamping fee is cash only, US dollars preferred - ATMs are landside, on the far side of immigration.
  • Photos missing. VOA needs two passport photos. No photo means a scramble at the counter.
  • Confusing VOA with visa exemption. Some nationalities need no visa at all - check that first before paying anyone.

Frequently asked questions

Is visa on arrival or e-visa better for Vietnam?

For most travelers the e-visa: cheaper, no letter service, no extra airport counter. VOA still wins for urgent trips (express letters in hours) and failed e-visa fallbacks.

How long is the visa on arrival queue?

The Landing Visa counter adds 20-40 minutes at peak before the main 45-90 minute immigration queue. An escort handling the paperwork cuts most of it.

Can I use fast track with a VOA letter?

Yes - arguably where it helps most. The agent works the Landing Visa counter for you, then walks you through priority immigration.

Does the e-visa work at every Vietnam airport?

At the port named on it. All international airports are covered, but you must enter through the listed port - verify it matches your flight.
